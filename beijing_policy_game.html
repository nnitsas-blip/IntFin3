Beijing Policy Game

You advise China’s economic leadership for four years. Each year you choose one policy. Your job is to shrink the trade surplus without wrecking growth, the public finances or relations with trading partners.

The four-year plan

Year 1 of 4
Lecture 4 slides 9–18, Exhibits 4.3–4.6; links to Lecture 4 (Part 2)

You start from China’s 2024 position: national saving 43.0% and investment 40.5% of GDP, a current account surplus of 2.5% of GDP. If you do nothing, the housing slump keeps cutting investment by 0.5 points a year and an ageing population keeps saving a little more, so the surplus widens. Remember CA = S − I. Each policy can be used once. Effect sizes are stylised teaching assumptions, not estimates.
